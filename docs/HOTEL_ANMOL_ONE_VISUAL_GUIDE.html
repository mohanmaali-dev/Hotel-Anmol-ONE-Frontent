Billing and payment

Always update money from Bill Details so the order, bill, and sale stay equal.

Paid

Paid amount equals the final amount. Due is zero.

Partial

Some money was received. The remaining amount stays due.

Not Paid

No money received yet. The complete amount stays due.

Quick answers

Why did stock not decrease after creating an order?

Stock decreases only when the order is marked Completed.

Why did stock not increase after creating a purchase?

Stock increases only when the purchase is marked Received.

Where do I record a payment received later?

Open the existing bill and select Update Payment. The order and sale update automatically.

Why can I not complete an order?

Check the insufficient-stock message. Add the missing ingredient or correct the recipe.

Why is a menu option missing?

The logged-in user may not have permission to view that module.
